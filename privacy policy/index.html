1. Information Handling & Local Processing

GalAI functions entirely as an offline-first utility application. We prioritize strict data minimization—your personal photos and device media never leave your device.

A. On-Device AI & Gallery Operations

GalAI features offline AI-powered gallery management, including smart photo organization, categorization, and duplicate detection. To provide these features, the app interacts with:

  • Device Photos and Media: Accessed strictly on your device local storage sandbox to enable automated sorting, cleanup, and indexing features.
  • Local Metadata: File attributes and structural paths are read locally to categorize images efficiently.
  • Offline Execution: All neural models and image analysis run locally on your device hardware. We do not upload, transmit, or back up any photos, videos, or album structures to any external cloud servers or remote databases.

B. Personal Information

GalAI does not require user accounts, logins, or cloud synchronization profiles, and does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, phone number, or precise location.

Integrated Third-Party Partners:

3. Children’s Privacy

GalAI is a general utility tool designed for productivity and gallery management. It does not knowingly solicit or archive personal data from children under the age of 13. If any third-party framework logs telemetry metrics from an underage user, standard operating system ad-tracking limitations apply.

Because all gallery processing and AI indexing happen entirely on-device, we hold no user media or personal files on external servers. If you wish to wipe any application cache or local indexes, you can easily clear app data from your Android device settings or simply uninstall the application, which removes all local app artifacts instantly.
